OVERLAPS TO WATCH

Luck: Lucky, Secret Hunter, Server Luck and Super Potion all boost rarity. You probably only need 2 of these.
Mutations: Mutation Luck vs the two Guaranteed ones. The Guaranteed ones are much stronger (1 min of 100% mutations).
Super Potion is Lucky + Money + Power + Fast Swing in one. If you keep it, those 4 feel less special.
Protection: Iron Skin and Second Chance do almost the same job.
Shooting: Power, Critical Hit, Fast Swing and Split Shot all just mean "break eggs faster". 2 of them is plenty.
