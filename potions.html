POTION SHOP

Restocks every 10 minutes, on the clock: xx:00, xx:10, xx:20... Every server uses the same real time (UTC), so all servers restock at the same moment, with a countdown in the shop.
Global stock: each restock rolls which potions are in stock and how many. Better potions have lower odds of showing up and less stock. Stock is shared by every server, and the same restock shows the same items everywhere.
Coins or Robux: every potion in stock can be bought with coins. Every potion can also be bought with Robux at any time, even when it's sold out.
Restock for 49 R$: instantly rerolls the shop just for you, with a fresh personal stock until the next global restock.
Global Restock for 699 R$: instantly restocks the shop for EVERY player in EVERY server, with a big announcement banner ("[Name] restocked the Potion Shop for everyone!"). The 10-minute timer starts again from then.
Using a potion while one is already active:
• Same potion, same strength: the time stacks (adds on).
• A stronger one (e.g. 3x Money while 2x Money is running): the stronger one takes over now, and the old one is saved with its remaining time. When the stronger one runs out, the saved one picks up where it left off.
• A weaker one while a stronger one runs: it's saved and starts once the stronger one ends.
Admin menu (F7): set the stock of any potion, reroll the stock now (all servers), and give yourself any potion for testing.

HOW THEY'D WORK

Stacking: the same potion stacks its time; a stronger one takes over and the old one waits (see Potion Shop above). Different potions all work at the same time.
Timers only count down while you're online, so nobody wastes a potion by leaving.
HUD: a row of small potion icons with countdowns above the hotbar.
Where to get them: every potion can be earned in-game: the coin Potion Shop (restocks every 10 minutes), rare drops from breaking eggs, Index rewards and Group rewards. The only Robux-only item is Server Luck, at the bottom of the shop.
+ R$ tag: every potion can ALSO be bought with Robux in the shop, for players who don't want to wait for the Potion Shop to restock.
